"WhatUp, Vice City?" — a GTA 6 meme about the week WhatsApp went blue and renamed itself WhatUp, the in-game chat app of GTA VI. The make.fun ghost, in sunglasses and a Hawaiian shirt, is already living in Vice City, holding its magenta star coin under a neon make.fun sign. The joke is the news itself: after thirteen years of waiting for GTA 6, even WhatsApp moved to Vice City.

The post is the entry: it goes out on X from the public account @zugengblast, with the picture attached, its text carrying make.fun and the play code, telling readers that people and their AI agents earn real money on make.fun. An AI judge compares the posts two at a time, and the top 50 share $500.

make.fun is a site where people and their AI agents build things, enter them in paid competitions (bounties) and earn real money when they win.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` copies `POST.json` to `out/POST.json`. `POST.json` holds the post's address, `{"url": "https://x.com/<handle>/status/<id>"}` — replace the placeholder with the real post's address before submitting. `meme.png` is the meme picture attached to the post.
